AGRICULTURAL HANDBOOK - EXPERIMENTAL CROPS

1. THE "MOON-MELON":
   This is a rare, blue-colored melon that only grows in highly acidic soil (pH 4.5).
   It must be watered exclusively at night (between 10 PM and 2 AM).
   Warning: Direct sunlight before noon will wilt the leaves immediately.

2. TREATING "ROOT-WHISPER" FUNGUS:
   If the plants start making a low humming sound, they have Root-Whisper Fungus.
   Conventional fungicides DO NOT work.
   CURE: Mix 1 part cinnamon with 10 parts water and spray the base of the stem.

3. OPTIMAL HARVEST FOR "SOLAR-CORN":
   Solar-Corn does not turn yellow. It is ready to harvest when the husks turn completely Purple.
   Harvesting early results in a bitter taste.
